Privacy Policy

DoseJournal  ·  Last updated: 31 July 2026

DoseJournal ("the app", "we", "our") is a personal medication tracking tool that helps individuals log and manage their own medications. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights as a user. Please read it carefully before using the app.

1. Data We Collect

To use DoseJournal, you must create an account. When you do, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address, used to create and authenticate your account
  • Medication data — medication names, doses, units, frequencies, and scheduled times that you enter
  • Dose logs — records of when you logged taking your medications, including timestamps
  • Optional photos — images of your medications if you choose to add them
  • Optional notes — indications, tablet appearance details, and other notes you add
  • Optional check-in responses — health and wellbeing check-in answers you choose to complete
  • App preferences — settings such as dark mode, notification preferences, and accent colour

All health-related data (medications, dose logs, photos, and notes) is linked to your account.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Your account and health data is stored securely using Supabase (supabase.com), a third-party database provider. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and encrypted at rest. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

App preferences and settings are also stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the core functionality of DoseJournal, including:

  • Displaying your medication schedule and dose history
  • Sending push notifications to remind you to take your medications
  • Calculating dose totals and countdowns for as-required medications
  • Generating insights and history views within the app

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app for you.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

DoseJournal does not collect or use:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA or equivalent)
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking data
  • Analytics sent to third-party advertising or analytics platforms
  • Data from Apple HealthKit or any other health platform
  • Financial or payment information

6. Push Notifications

If you enable medication reminders, DoseJournal uses push notifications delivered through Apple's Push Notification Service (APNs). Notification content is generated locally on your device or on our notification server and is not shared with third parties. You can disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings.

7. Third-Party Services

DoseJournal uses the following third-party service when cloud features are enabled:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy

We do not integrate any advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or data brokers.

8. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete the app or clear its data. If you use cloud sync, your data is retained in our database until you request deletion. You can request deletion of all your data at any time by contacting us at the address below.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Medical Disclaimer

DoseJournal is a personal tracking tool only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations. All medication information is entered by the user based on their own prescriber or pharmacist's instructions. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare provider regarding your medications.
